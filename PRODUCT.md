# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Discerning local and international travelers seeking a blend of high-end luxury, nature-focused relaxation, and eco-adventures in Kitulgala, Sri Lanka. Visitors include couples looking for a romantic getaway, families, and adventure seekers.

## Product Purpose
The official homepage of RiverSide Paradise Resort, designed to establish brand presence, immerse visitors in the resort's luxury tropical riverbank atmosphere, showcase highlights and package details, and drive direct booking conversions.

## Brand Personality
- **Peaceful**: Serene, calming, and deeply integrated with nature.
- **Luxurious**: Premium, elegant, and highly polished with great attention to detail.
- **Authentic**: Reflective of Sri Lankan hospitality, cultural warmth, and local adventure.

## Anti-references
- Generic flat tech SaaS layouts.
- Over-rounded card styling (`border-radius > 16px`).
- Tiny tracked uppercase eyebrows above every single section (the standard AI template look).
- Side-stripe borders as card accents.
- Gradient text styles.
- Low-contrast, washed-out grey body copy on off-white backgrounds.

## Design Principles
- **Editorial Luxury**: Clean layouts, high-contrast typography, and generous negative space reminiscent of a high-end travel magazine.
- **Immersive River-scape**: Dominant nature photography with deep, rich greens, sand tones, and river blues that convey a sense of place.
- **Natural Fluidity**: Smooth transitions, responsive grids, and subtle micro-interactions that mirror the gentle flow of water.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.
- Maintain text contrast ratios of at least 4.5:1 (including overlays and button copy).
- Support standard screen reader layout hierarchies and keyboard accessibility.
- Support `prefers-reduced-motion` settings for all scroll animations and slider movements.
